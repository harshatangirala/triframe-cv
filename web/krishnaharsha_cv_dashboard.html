Computer Vision with Deep Learning Project Dashboard

TriFrame tests whether weekly, daily and hourly chart images add information about a US stock's next-20-day volatility, drawdown risk and 5-day relative direction beyond plain numbers from the same price history. Seven frozen, ImageNet-pretrained vision models were compared with a numerical baseline on 979 US stocks, with chronological training, validation and test periods and a sealed final holdout.

How to use: upload three TradingView screenshots of one stock (1W, 1D and 1H). The app reads the ticker and the last bar of each picture, checks them against market data, rebuilds the exact inputs the model was trained on and returns expected volatility, the chance of a 10% drawdown, the chance of beating the median stock over five days, and a NO TRADE decision (the default).

Test-period results (2020-2022): volatility R² 0.50; drawdown AUC 0.66; direction AUC 0.510. Research output, not investment advice.
